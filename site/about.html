About PickleballRx

Pickleball RX was started by Don, Beni and Siva (@picklebrx). We review pickleball paddles from the inside out: X-rays, microscope grit scans and spin measured at 960 frames per second. (Placeholder: add a few sentences about your playing background.)

How we test

  1. Play it. Every paddle gets drills and real games, and a video review on YouTube.
  2. Measure it. Weight, swing weight, twist weight, dimensions and core thickness, measured ourselves.
  3. Look inside. X-rays show the core and ring. A 4K microscope shows face grit and wear.
  4. Measure spin. A fixed spin rig filmed at 960 fps, then re-tested after real play.
  5. Score it. Power, spin, control and forgiveness on the same 10-point scale.
