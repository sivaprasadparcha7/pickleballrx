How our Rx algorithm works

Every paddle gets four ratings from 0 to 10: power, spin, defense (control and fast hands) and forgiveness (sweet spot). Where we've tested a paddle, our numbers win:

  • Spin comes from our Spin Lab average RPM when we've tested it. Otherwise it starts at 6 and rises if another reviewer ranks it highly for spin.
  • Power rises for full foam (a little more for EPP), elongated shapes, 14 mm cores and a higher measured swing weight from our Spec Lab.
  • Defense rises for Gen 3 honeycomb, 16 mm cores, wider shapes, a lighter measured swing weight and a higher twist weight.
  • Forgiveness rises for wide and hybrid shapes, 16 mm cores and a higher measured twist weight.
  • Our own picks (the Leader Board and top picks) nudge the matching rating up.

Then your answers turn into a match score out of 100:

  • Up to 55 points for your priority rating. All-court uses the average of power, spin and defense, minus a little for a lopsided paddle.
  • Up to 20 points for your level. Beginners get forgiveness and defense, intermediates get a balance, advanced players get power and spin.
  • Up to 15 points for evidence: our X-ray, Spin Lab or Spec Lab data, our own picks, and public picks from John Kew, Matt's Pickleball, Pickleball Effect, Supasenpai and The Kitchen.
  • Up to 10 points for fit: in your budget range, a confirmed hybrid top style, and brands with a local presence in your country.

Budget, construction, foam type and shape are filters: a paddle has to match them to be ranked. If too few paddles match, we loosen the least important filter and tell you which one. Discount codes never change the ranking. Tick “Only show brands we partner with” to limit the list to our partner brands; where we have a code it shows on the paddle.

Specs and prices come from each brand's store (checked Nov 2026) and can change. Where a brand doesn't say the foam type or hybrid top style, we mark it unknown rather than guess. 88 of the 417 paddles have our own lab data; 86 appear on another reviewer's public list; 9 of 79 hybrids have a confirmed top style.
