Brand spotlight: Gearbox Pickleball

Passion, engineered.

Rafael Filippini, Gearbox's founder and CEO, is one of the most passionate people in the paddle business. He went from pro racquetball and the Marines to building paddles in San Diego with his own patented carbon cores, and he calls his team "extremely passionate about what we do." His Pressure X 8E holds the top spot on our Leader Board.

Rafael's story

Rafael Filippini moved to the U.S. from Mexico as a young child, before he spoke English. He joined the Marines, where he first worked with composites on high-performance aircraft, then studied engineering while playing professional racquetball. After 13 years with a racquetball equipment maker, he founded Gearbox in 2007 as a racquetball racquet company.

He first saw pickleball in 2009 in St. George, Utah, where older players beat him and his friends. Gearbox added pickleball paddles in 2015, and Rafael set out to give players "an actual edge" rather than a new color or logo. Today Gearbox sells in close to 30 countries, is the official paddle of the National Pickleball League, and holds a long list of its own patents, including the SST carbon-rib core we see in our X-rays.

Sources: The Dink and the San Diego Business Journal.
