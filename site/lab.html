X-Ray Rx

What's inside 56 pickleball paddles.

We X-ray each paddle and label what's inside: core, perimeter ring, inserts and handle, alongside the published specs and where they came from.

Labels are our read of the X-ray, not a cutaway. Measurements are approximate. Disclaimers.

8

Honeycomb cells are about 7 to 8.5 mm

Nearly every honeycomb core we've measured falls in that range. The Vanwin Alpha Control and the 11SIX24 Hurache-X Alpha Pro Power, both at about 10 mm, are the only large-cell paddles so far.

F

Foam has taken over

41 of 56 paddles have no honeycomb in the face: full foam cores (EPP, MPP, EVA or dual-density), usually inside a denser perimeter ring.

R

Rings range from about 7 to 28 mm

Perimeter rings vary a lot, from about 7 to 8 mm (Jelly Bean 2, Kamito Dominus) to 26 to 28 mm (Facolos Elite X 2.0, Luzz Inferno 2).
