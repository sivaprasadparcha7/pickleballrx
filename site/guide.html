Buying guide

How to choose a pickleball paddle

PickleballRx team

The best paddle is the one that matches how you play. Start with these questions, or let the paddle finder do it for you.

1. How heavy should it be?

Lighter paddles (roughly under 7.8 oz) are quicker at the kitchen line and easier on the arm. Heavier paddles (over about 8.2 oz) add stability and drive power but can tire you out. Swing weight matters even more than static weight: it measures how heavy the paddle feels when you swing it.

2. What is twist weight?

Twist weight measures how much a paddle resists twisting on off-center hits. Higher twist weight means a more forgiving, stable paddle.

3. What core thickness?

Thicker cores (around 16 mm) tend to feel softer and more controlled. Thinner cores (around 13 to 14 mm) usually feel crisper with more pop.

4. Which shape?

  • Standard / wide body: biggest sweet spot, most forgiving.
  • Elongated: more reach and power, smaller sweet spot.
  • Hybrid: a middle ground.

5. What face material?

Fiberglass faces often feel springier. Carbon fiber faces usually give more control and, with a textured surface, more spin. Our microscope scans show how much grit a face really has.

Check your league's rules: sanctioned events require approved paddles, so look for an approval mark before you buy.
