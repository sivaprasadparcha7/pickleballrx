Their story

In Speedup's own words: "We're Santhosh and Mac — founders, engineers, and competitive pickleball players." The brand "started with a simple goal: design high-performance paddles from first principles, not by copying what already exists." Santhosh is a professional player with international medals across Australia, Japan, India and Vietnam, and Mac is a 5.0-rated player and "a gear obsessive." Their aim is to build "paddles we would choose to play with — designed carefully, tested thoroughly, and priced responsibly."
