Proton

Series One: Project Bluejay and Project Cardinal

Pre-order

Elongated, 15 mm · $295 pre-order price

Proton's next paddles come in two colorways, Bluejay and Cardinal, both elongated at 15 mm. The new paddle was in the hands of pros at the Pickleball World Championships in early November, including Meghan Dizon, Zane Ford and Naomi Nguyen. Proton hasn't published full specs yet.

Selkirk

Boomstik ST with Sharktooth Groove Technology

Just released

Released Nov 10, 2026 · $350

The next version of the LABS Project Boomstik. Selkirk says its new InfiniGrit Ultra surface has four times the spin friction and twice the grit durability of the original InfiniGrit. It adds a perimeter Sharktooth groove and hand-adjustable perimeter weights, and keeps the BoomCore foam core.
