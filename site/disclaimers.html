Last updated 2026-11-16. Here is how our results are made, who supplied what, and where the limits are.

Discount codes and commission

Pickleball RX has discount codes with some brands, including Arronax, Evolv, Gearbox, Holbrook, Luzz, MARK, Pakle and RPM, and may earn a commission when you buy with a code or through some links. It costs you nothing extra. Deals and prices are checked on the brand's own site when we post them, but they can change or end at any time, so confirm the final price at checkout.

Brands have no input into results

No brand sees, edits or approves our results before they are published. Having a code with a brand never changes a number, a score or a ranking, and a brand can't pay for a spot on the Leader Board.

Paddles supplied by brands

  • Evolv requested the CarbonSkyn spin durability test and supplied the three Origin paddles used in it. The testing and the numbers are our own.
  • The Grit E2CF paddles were supplied by the owner of Grit.
  • Grit E1 CW and E1 TW are recreational-play paddles that are not USA Pickleball approved.

Spin Lab

Spin numbers come from our own static rig: the same ball machine and setup, filmed at 960 frames per second. It is not an official USA Pickleball test. Results depend on the ball, the impact angle and where the ball hits the face, so treat small gaps between paddles as a tie, and compare paddles within our table rather than against other testers' numbers.

X-Ray Rx

X-ray labels (core, perimeter ring, inserts, handle) are our interpretation of the X-ray image. They are not a cutaway, and brands have not confirmed them. Ring widths and other measurements taken from an X-ray are estimates.

Grit Lab

Microscope images were taken with a 4K digital microscope. Wear percentages are conservative visual estimates, about plus or minus 10 points, made by comparing each paddle's hitting zone with its own throat under matched light. They are not profilometer measurements, and grit wears at different rates depending on how much and how you play.

Specs

Swing weight, twist weight and static weight marked as ours are measured by Pickleball RX. Other specs come from the sources listed with each paddle, usually the brand or a retailer. Paddles vary from unit to unit and brands update models, so your paddle can differ from what we list.

Not professional advice

Everything on this site is our own testing and opinion, for general information only. It is not professional advice. Check specs, approval status and warranty terms with the brand before you buy, and check the current USA Pickleball approved list before using a paddle in sanctioned play.
