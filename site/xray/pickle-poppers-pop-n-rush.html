Quick take

The Pickle Poppers Pop N Rush is an elongated pickleball paddle from Pickle Poppers. Our X-ray shows the core: MPPE foam (white density foam). Perimeter: TPE 11–22 mm (top ≈ 9.5) + EVA ≈ 11 mm. Face: T700 carbon + MAGMAGRIT grit. Weight: 7.9 oz (± 0.2). In our Spin Lab it averaged 2,025 RPM (best 2,095 RPM).
