Quick take

The Speedup Tide Elongated (14L) is a 14 mm elongated pickleball paddle from Speedup. Our X-ray shows the core: MPP full foam (PureFeel). Perimeter: TPE ≈ 13 mm + injection foam ≈ 10.5 mm. Face: FlexGrid 45° carbon fibre, peel ply. Weight: 8.35 oz (static). In our Spin Lab it averaged 1,935 RPM (best 2,070 RPM). Its Arm Score is 110 (reference paddle = 100; higher is easier on the arm) on a used paddle.
