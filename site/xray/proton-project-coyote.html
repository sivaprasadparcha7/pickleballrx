Quick take

The Proton Project Coyote is a 15 mm elongated pickleball paddle from Proton. Our X-ray shows the core: Polypropylene (PP) honeycomb. Perimeter: Foam-injected ≈ 20 mm, type n/d. Face: Aerospace carbon, roughened. Weight: 7.7–7.78 oz.
