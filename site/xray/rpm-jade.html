Quick take

The RPM Jade is a pickleball paddle from RPM. Our X-ray shows the core: MPP full foam (no honeycomb). Perimeter: EVA ring ≈ 25 mm. Face: SICmatrix silicon carbide. Weight: 7.7–7.8 oz. In our Spin Lab it averaged 1,785 RPM (best 1,930 RPM). Its Arm Score is 133 (reference paddle = 100; higher is easier on the arm).
